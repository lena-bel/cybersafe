WHY CYBERSECURITY?

Your information is valuable.

Every day, we share personal information online. Simple habits can make a big difference in protecting ourselves against digital threats.

LEARN THE BASICS

Protect yourself online.

5 security tips available

01

Strong passwords

Use long and unique passwords for your accounts. Avoid using the same password everywhere.

02

Beware of phishing

Never click suspicious links. Always verify the sender before opening an email.

03

Protect your privacy

Think before sharing personal information on social media and other websites.

04

Update your devices

Keep your applications and operating system updated to improve your security.

05

Secure your Wi-Fi

Use a strong Wi-Fi password and be careful when connecting to public networks.

THINK BEFORE YOU CLICK

Not everything online is what it seems.

Suspicious messages, fake websites and unknown downloads can put your personal information at risk. Stay alert and make informed decisions.
